Cowboy Action Shooting

A SASS-affiliated cowboy action club shooting Range 3, with matches twice a month, rain or shine.

RangeRange 3
SanctioningSASS
Schedule1st Sunday, 8:30am–3:00pm
Also3rd Wednesday, 9:00am–4:00pm

Run by the Cavalier Cowboys

This match is organized by the Cavalier Cowboys, a SASS-affiliated club that keeps its own site with full rules, match history, scores, and a gallery — plus an annual SASS Virginia State Championship hosted right here on Range 3.

  • Single Action Shooting Society (SASS) rules and categories apply.
  • Look for the cowboy hats on Range 3.
  • 10 mph speed limit on Boondock Lane at all times, out of courtesy to neighbors.
Entry fees and a named match director aren't published by the Cowboys — their contact form is the reliable way to reach them.
