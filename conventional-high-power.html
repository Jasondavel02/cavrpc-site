Public Match

Conventional High Power

An NRA-rules reduced-course rifle match on Range 5, shot at 200 yards against National Match-style targets. Open to the public most months of the year.

RangeRange 5
Check-in8:00 AM — match starts 9:00 AM
2026 datesMar 28 · May 23 · Aug 22 · Oct 24 · Dec 26
Eligible riflesNRA Service & Match Rifle

Course of fire

1Offhand slow fire — 2 sighters + 20 rounds in 20 min, SR target
2Rapid fire sitting — 2 sighters + 2×10 rounds, 60 sec/string, SR target
3Rapid fire prone — 2 sighters + 2×10 rounds, 70 sec/string, SR-42 (300yd reduced to 200)
4Slow fire prone — 2 sighters + 20 rounds in 20 min, MR-52 (600yd reduced to 200)

Rules & equipment

  • Current NRA rules and NRA classification apply.
  • Open to NRA Service Rifles (Rule 3.1 a–e) and NRA Match Rifles, metallic or any-sight (Rule 3.3).
  • Electronic targets are used — bring a tablet or other web-capable device to score.
