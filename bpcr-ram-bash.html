Club Match — Members & Invited Guests

BPCR Ram Bash

Black-powder cartridge rifle silhouette, offhand at 200 yards, on Range 5's covered firing line — rain or shine.

RangeRange 5 (covered line)
Schedule1st Saturday, monthly
Check-inPractice 9:00am · match 10:00am
Fee$20

Course of fire

40 shots offhand at a full-size 500m ram, shot at 200 yards. Bring a spotting scope if you have one.

Rules & equipment

  • Any rifle designed or chambered for a blackpowder metallic cartridge, or a typical reproduction — except Schuetzen or bolt action.
  • Period metallic sights allowed; no slings, scopes, or tube sights.
  • Fixed cartridges .32 caliber or larger, cast or swaged lead bullet, safe smokeless powder/blackpowder/Pyrodex/duplex charge.
  • No jacketed or gas-check bullets allowed.
Limited capacity — preregister by email or phone. Walk-ins are accepted match day if space allows.
